Not a wolf

August 29, 2026

Text:

As a kid, I thought about wolves all the time. Unlike humans, who were weak, driven by selfishness and fear on nearly indistinguishable trajectories, wolves had absolute freedom. They might be good or evil, but I had the sense that they were noble at their core.

My friend introduced me to WolfQuest, a game where you’re a wolf who’s just left your natal pack and must learn to survive on your own. You explore the mountains, hunt elk, find a mate, birth pups, while the seasons change.

Soon, you must seek new territory, which lies beyond a gushing stream, too wide for your pups to cross alone. A hawk flies overhead, hungry, talons at the ready, and your friend told you she never made it across with all her pups alive.

If you were a wolf, you would do it anyway. But you’re just a human, weak and fearful, so you stop the game there, and you never do cross the stream.
